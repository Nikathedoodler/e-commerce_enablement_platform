# Demo Walkthrough Script

**Purpose:** Guide for demonstrating the platform to pilot users  
**Duration:** 15-20 minutes  
**Audience:** Pilot users (e-commerce brands/retailers)

---

## 🎯 Demo Objectives

By the end of this demo, pilot users should understand:

- How to manage inventory and track stock levels
- How to create and fulfill orders
- How to receive new inventory
- How the platform helps streamline their fulfillment operations

---

## 📋 Pre-Demo Checklist

Before starting the demo:

- [ ] All demo assets are loaded (10 inventory items, 5 orders, 4 receiving logs)
- [ ] Browser is ready with the dashboard open
- [ ] Have the demo account logged in
- [ ] Test that all pages load correctly
- [ ] Have user guides ready to reference if needed

---

## 🎬 Demo Script

### Introduction (2 minutes)

**Opening:**
"Welcome! Today I'll show you how our e-commerce enablement platform can help streamline your fulfillment operations. We'll walk through the main features using sample data so you can see how everything works together."

**What to Cover:**

- Brief overview of the platform's purpose
- Main sections: Orders, Inventory, Receiving, Settings
- Emphasize: "This is your central hub for managing fulfillment"

---

### 1. Dashboard Overview (1 minute)

**Navigate to:** Dashboard home page

**What to Show:**

- Clean, organized interface
- Sidebar navigation
- Main content area

**What to Say:**
"This is your dashboard. Everything you need is accessible from the sidebar on the left. Let's start by looking at your inventory."

---

### 2. Inventory Management (4-5 minutes)

**Navigate to:** Inventory → All Items

**What to Show:**

- 10 demo inventory items
- Low stock alerts (red badges)
- Search functionality
- Item details (SKU, quantity, location, reorder threshold)

**What to Say:**
"Here's your inventory. You can see all your products at a glance. Notice the red badges - those are low stock alerts. When quantity falls below the reorder threshold, you'll see a warning so you can restock before running out."

**Key Points to Emphasize:**

- ✅ Low stock alerts help prevent stockouts
- ✅ Location tracking helps find items quickly
- ✅ Search makes it easy to find specific products

**Navigate to:** Inventory → Low Stock

**What to Show:**

- Filtered view showing only low stock items
- 5 items with red status

**What to Say:**
"You can also view just your low stock items here. This makes it easy to see what needs restocking at a glance."

**Navigate to:** Inventory → Add New (optional, just show the form)

**What to Say:**
"Adding new inventory is simple - just fill in the SKU, name, quantity, and set a reorder threshold. The system will automatically alert you when stock gets low."

---

### 3. Orders Management (5-6 minutes)

**Navigate to:** Orders → All Orders

**What to Show:**

- 5 demo orders
- Different order statuses (pending, processing, fulfilled, cancelled)
- Order details (customer, total, items, status)

**What to Say:**
"Here are your orders. You can see orders in different stages - pending orders that need to be fulfilled, orders being processed, and completed orders. Let's look at a specific order."

**Click on:** DEMO-ORD-001 (Pending order)

**What to Show:**

- Order detail dialog
- Customer information
- Shipping address
- Order items with quantities and prices

**What to Say:**
"Here's the order detail. You can see everything you need to fulfill it - customer info, shipping address, and the items. You can update the order status as you work through fulfillment."

**Key Points to Emphasize:**

- ✅ All order information in one place
- ✅ Easy status updates (Pending → Processing → Fulfilled)
- ✅ Track order progress through the fulfillment pipeline

**Navigate to:** Orders → Pending

**What to Show:**

- Filtered view of pending orders
- 2 pending orders

**What to Say:**
"Here's your pending orders - these are the ones that need attention. You can filter by status to focus on what needs to be done."

**Navigate to:** Orders → Create Order (optional, just show the form)

**What to Say:**
"You can also create orders manually if needed. Just fill in the customer details, shipping address, and add the items. The system calculates the total automatically."

### 4. Receiving Workflow (3-4 minutes)

**Navigate to:** Receiving

**What to Show:**

**What to Say:**

"You can record the condition of items - good items automatically update your inventory, while damaged items are logged but don't affect stock levels. This helps you track quality issues."

## ❓ Common Questions & Answers

**Q: Can I import my existing inventory?**  
A: Yes, we support CSV import. You can bulk upload your inventory items.

**Q: What happens if I run out of stock?**  
A: The system alerts you when stock falls below your reorder threshold, so you can restock before running out.

**Q: Can I connect multiple Shopify stores?**  
A: Yes, you can connect multiple stores. Each store's orders will appear in your orders list.

**Q: How do I generate shipping labels?**  
A: You can generate shipping labels directly from order details. We integrate with DHL for label generation.

**Q: What if I need to cancel an order?**  
A: You can update the order status to "cancelled" and update the financial status to "refunded" if needed.

**Q: Can I track inventory by location?**  
A: Yes, you can assign locations to inventory items and filter by location when receiving new stock.

**Q: What happens to damaged items?**  
A: When you receive items in "damaged" condition, they're logged but don't update your inventory. This helps you track quality issues.

**Q: How do I know if I'm approaching my order limit?**  
A: You'll see warnings when creating orders if you're approaching your plan's limit. Check your Billing page for current usage.

## 💡 Demo Tips

### Do's ✅

- **Keep it conversational** - Ask if they have questions as you go
- **Show real workflows** - Demonstrate actual tasks they'll do
- **Highlight pain points solved** - "This solves the problem of..."
- **Be enthusiastic** - Show your passion for the product
- **Pause for questions** - Don't rush through everything

### Don'ts ❌

- **Don't read from the script** - Use it as a guide, not a script
- **Don't show everything** - Focus on what's most relevant
- **Don't ignore questions** - Address them even if it means skipping ahead
- **Don't apologize for features** - If something isn't perfect, acknowledge it but focus on what works
- **Don't rush** - Give them time to absorb information
