# Pilot Quick Start Guide

**Welcome to the Pilot Program!** This quick guide will get you up and running in about 15 minutes.

---

## 🎯 What You Can Do in This Pilot

- **Manage Inventory**: Track products, quantities, and get low stock alerts
- **Handle Orders**: Create orders manually or sync from Shopify
- **Generate Shipping Labels**: Create DHL shipping labels for your orders
- **Receive Inventory**: Log incoming inventory and automatically update stock
- **Connect Shopify**: Sync orders automatically from your Shopify store

---

## ⚡ Quick Setup (5 minutes)

### 1. Log In

1. Go to [your-platform-url]
2. Log in with your pilot credentials
3. You'll see your dashboard

### 2. Complete Your Profile

1. Click **Settings** → **Profile** (in the sidebar)
2. Verify your name and company name
3. Click "Save"

**That's it!** You're ready to start using the platform.

---

## 🚀 Your First Tasks (10 minutes)

### Task 1: Add Your Inventory (5 minutes)

**Why:** Track your products and get low stock alerts

**How:**
1. Go to **Inventory** → **Add New**
2. Fill in:
   - **SKU**: Your product identifier (e.g., "PROD-001")
   - **Name**: Product name
   - **Quantity**: Current stock level
   - **Reorder Threshold**: Alert when stock drops below this (e.g., "10")
   - **Location**: Optional warehouse location
3. Click "Create Item"
4. Repeat for your main products

**Tip:** Start with your top 5-10 products. You can add more later.

---

### Task 2: Create or Import Your First Order (5 minutes)

**Option A: Create Order Manually**
1. Go to **Orders** → **Create Order**
2. Fill in customer and order details
3. Add items and click "Create Order"

**Option B: Connect Shopify (Recommended)**
1. Go to **Settings** → **Integrations**
2. Click "Connect Shopify Store"
3. Enter your store domain (e.g., `yourstore.myshopify.com`)
4. Authorize the connection
5. Orders from Shopify will appear automatically!

---

## 📋 Daily Workflow

Once you're set up, here's your typical daily workflow:

### Morning Checklist
- [ ] Check **Orders** → **Pending** for new orders
- [ ] Check **Inventory** → **Low Stock** for items needing restock

### Fulfilling an Order
1. Go to **Orders** → Click on an order
2. Review order details and items
3. Click **"Generate Label"**
4. Fill in package weight and dimensions
5. Download and print the label
6. Update order status to "Fulfilled" when shipped

### Receiving Inventory
1. Go to **Receiving**
2. Fill in the receiving form:
   - SKU and quantity
   - Condition (usually "good")
   - Location
   - Supplier/client (optional)
3. Click "Log Receipt"
4. Inventory is updated automatically!

## 💡 Key Features to Try

### Low Stock Alerts
- Go to **Inventory** → **Low Stock**

### Order Status Tracking
- Use order statuses: Pending → Processing → Fulfilled
- Track order progress through the fulfillment pipeline

### Search & Filter
- Use search bars to quickly find orders or inventory items
- Filter orders by status (Pending, Fulfilled, etc.)
