## Account Setup

### 1. Create Your Account

1. Visit the [Sign Up page](/auth/signup)
2. Fill in your information:
   - **Full Name**: Your name
   - **Company Name**: Your business name
   - **Email**: Your business email address
   - **Password**: Create a strong password
3. Click "Create Account"
4. Check your email for a confirmation link
5. Click the confirmation link to verify your account

### 2. Complete Your Profile

After logging in for the first time:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Profile**
2. Verify your information is correct
3. Update your company name if needed

## Dashboard Overview

Your dashboard is organized into several main sections:

### Navigation Menu

- **Orders**: Manage all your orders
  - All Orders: View all orders
  - Pending: Orders awaiting fulfillment
  - Fulfilled: Completed orders
  - Create Order: Manually create a new order

- **Inventory**: Track your products
  - All Items: View all inventory items
  - Low Stock: Items that need restocking

- **Receiving**: Log incoming inventory
  - Record new inventory arrivals
  - View receiving history

- **Settings**: Configure your account
  - Profile: Update your information
  - Integrations: Connect Shopify stores
  - Billing: Manage subscription and payment

## First Steps

### Step 1: Set Up Your Inventory

Before you can fulfill orders, you need to add your products:

1. Go to **Inventory** → **Add New**
2. Fill in product details:
   - **SKU**: Unique product identifier
   - **Name**: Product name
   - **Quantity**: Current stock level
   - **Reorder Threshold**: Minimum stock before alert
   - **Location**: Warehouse location
3. Click "Create Item"
4. Repeat for all your products

**Tip**: You can also add inventory through the Receiving module when items arrive at your warehouse.

### Step 2: Connect Your Shopify Store (Optional)

If you use Shopify, connect your store to automatically sync orders:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Integrations**
2. Click "Connect Shopify Store"
3. Enter your Shopify store domain (e.g., `mystore.myshopify.com`)
4. Authorize the connection
5. Your orders will now sync automatically!

See the [Shopify Integration Guide](./shopify-integration.md) for detailed instructions.

### Step 3: Create or Import Your First Order

**Option A: Manual Order Creation**

1. Go to **Orders** → **Create Order**
2. Fill in order details:
   - Customer email
   - Shipping address
   - Order items (SKU, quantity, price)
3. Click "Create Order"

**Option B: Shopify Orders (Automatic)**

If you've connected Shopify, orders will appear automatically in your dashboard when customers place orders.

### Step 4: Fulfill Your First Order

1. Go to **Orders** → **All Orders**
2. Click on an order to view details
3. Click "Generate Label" to create a shipping label
4. Fill in package details (weight, dimensions, service type)
5. Download and print the label
6. Update order status to "Fulfilled" when shipped

### Common Questions

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions.

**Q: Can I connect multiple Shopify stores?**
A: Yes! You can connect multiple stores from the Integrations page.

**Q: How are shipping costs calculated?**
A: Shipping costs are calculated by DHL based on package weight, dimensions, destination, and service type.

**Q: What happens if I exceed my plan's order limit?**
A: You'll see a warning on the billing page. Upgrade your plan or wait for the next billing period.
